Modern foundations.

Par-N-Rar 1.3 dates to January 2008. Its core idea still makes sense: monitor folders, verify and repair PAR/PAR2 sets, extract multipart RARs and let the user get on with something else. ParForge is an independent clean-room implementation of that workflow for current Windows systems.

PARFORGE

2026 rebuild

Windows 10/11 x64, current par2cmdline engine, stable-file detection, modern queue/history, standard 7-Zip integration and safe non-destructive defaults.

Why not just use MultiPar?

MultiPar is actively maintained and is a powerful PAR/PAR2 creation, verification and repair utility. ParForge is not trying to replace it at that job. ParForge targets the hands-off automation layer: keep an eye on one or more folders, wait until a set is complete, repair it, extract it, record what happened and move on.

Why local software instead of a SaaS?

Large archives should not need to be uploaded to a stranger's server just to repair or unpack them. ParForge runs the heavy work on your machine and has no account or telemetry. A future optional remote dashboard could still be added without putting your archive data in the cloud.
